Professional Standards

The Los Angeles Fire Department is committed to serving the residents of Los Angeles with courage, integrity, professionalism, and pride.

The Professional Standards Division helps ensure that Department employees uphold the highest standards of conduct and service while carrying out their responsibilities to the community.

Professional Standards Review

All submissions are reviewed appropriately and handled in accordance with Department policies and procedures. The Department takes concerns regarding employee conduct and service seriously and is committed to maintaining public trust.
