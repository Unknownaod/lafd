Submit Requests Online

Use the online portal below to submit a request for eligible Los Angeles Fire Department records. Select the request type that best matches your needs and follow the instructions provided.

Important: Medical and EMS records are handled separately. Please do not submit medical information through a fire incident records request.

Before You Submit

Please make sure your request contains enough information for staff to identify the record you are seeking.

  • Incident date, if known
  • Incident location, if known
  • Type of record requested
  • Your contact information

Request Processing

Submitted requests are reviewed before records are released. Additional information may be requested when necessary.

Some records may be withheld or redacted when required by applicable laws, privacy requirements, or active investigations.
