FIRE SAFETY

RED FLAG PARKING

To increase public safety, the Fire Department and Department of Transportation have created a program to remove illegally parked vehicles in posted locations within the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones.

The goal is to keep roadways clear and allow emergency vehicles to respond quickly during fast-moving brush fires.

FIRE PREVENTION

BRUSH

The canyons and hillsides of Los Angeles present unique fire-safety challenges. Brush, vegetation, and other hazards can contribute to the spread of wildfire.

The Los Angeles Fire Department works year-round to promote fire safety and help protect communities.
