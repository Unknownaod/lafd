Contact the Los Angeles Fire Department

We welcome your questions, comments and concerns. There are a number of ways to contact the Los Angeles Fire Department.

Emergency & Breaking News

To report an emergency, call 9-1-1. For immediate emergencies requiring police, fire or medical assistance, always contact 9-1-1.
Breaking News, Emergency Incident Information & Interviews LAFD Media Desk
Available every day, 7 AM to 10 PM

Media Relations

Non-Emergency Media Inquiries, Feature Stories & Interview Requests

Monday–Friday, business hours

To request an interview, feature story, or media event with LAFD, please email LAFDrequest@lafdi10.org with the following:

  • Story Overview – a brief description of your story and how LAFD factors in
  • Deadline – the date and time you need a response or information by
  • Specific Request – a statement, interview, access, data, or something else

Most requests are scheduled in advance to ensure proper staffing and accommodation. Please allow 48–72 hours for non-emergency requests to be processed.
