Department Executive Leadership

The Office of the Fire Chief provides executive leadership and administrative oversight for the Los Angeles Fire Department. The office establishes departmental priorities, oversees operational administration, and provides direction to the department's command staff and personnel. The following directory identifies the department's executive leadership and established chain of command for this FiveM roleplay organization.

Office of the Fire Chief

The Fire Chief serves as the department's senior executive authority. The Deputy Chief assists with department-wide administration and executive oversight, while Assistant Chiefs support command-level administration and the implementation of departmental directives.

Deputy Chief of Department

The Deputy Chief serves as the second-ranking executive officer and assists the Fire Chief with department-wide administration, command oversight, and implementation of departmental directives. The Deputy Chief may act on behalf of the Fire Chief when delegated authority.
