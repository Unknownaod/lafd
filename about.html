About LAFD

The Los Angeles Fire Department is dedicated to protecting the lives and property of the community through professional emergency response, fire suppression, rescue operations, emergency medical services, and public safety.

Our personnel work around the clock responding to emergencies and providing assistance to residents, visitors, and businesses throughout the city.

Through teamwork, training, professionalism, and commitment to service, the department strives to provide a high standard of emergency services to the community.

Our Mission

To protect life, property, and the environment through professional emergency response, prevention, education, and community service.

Our Services

Fire Suppression

Respond to structure fires, vehicle fires, wildland incidents, and other fire-related emergencies.

Emergency Medical Services

Provide emergency medical assistance and transport for individuals experiencing medical emergencies.

Rescue Operations

Respond to vehicle collisions, technical rescues, hazardous situations, and other incidents requiring specialized assistance.

Fire Prevention

Work to reduce fire risks through inspections, education, prevention programs, and enforcement.

Emergency Communications

Coordinate emergency calls and department resources to ensure personnel are dispatched where they are needed.

Community Outreach

Work with members of the community through public education, safety programs, and community events.

Our Values

Professionalism

We maintain a high standard of conduct, training, and performance while serving the community.

Integrity

We act honestly, responsibly, and ethically in every situation.

Teamwork

Emergency response depends on personnel working together and supporting one another.

Service

Our priority is serving and protecting the community we are entrusted with.
