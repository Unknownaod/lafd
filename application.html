Job Number: LAFD-FF-001

Job Overview

The Firefighter position is responsible for responding to emergency incidents, protecting life and property, performing fire suppression operations, and providing emergency assistance to the community.

Responsibilities

  • Respond to emergency incidents.
  • Perform fire suppression operations.
  • Assist with rescue operations.
  • Provide emergency medical assistance.
  • Operate and maintain department equipment.
  • Work effectively as part of an emergency response team.

General Requirements

  • Meet the minimum age requirement.
  • Possess a valid driver's license.
  • Maintain a professional standard of conduct.
  • Successfully complete required testing.
  • Successfully complete department training.

Additional Information

Applicants should review all requirements carefully before submitting an application. Additional information may be requested during the recruitment process.
