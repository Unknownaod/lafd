Evacuation Information

Be prepared to leave safely during an emergency

Emergency Evacuation

Wildfires and other emergencies can develop quickly. Having an evacuation plan before an emergency occurs can help protect you, your family, pets, and property.

Residents should remain aware of changing conditions and follow instructions from emergency officials.

If You Are Ordered to Evacuate Leave immediately using the recommended evacuation route. Do not wait for conditions to worsen or for emergency personnel to arrive at your home.

Ready, Set, Go!

R

READY

Prepare your household, emergency supplies, important documents, vehicles, and pets before an evacuation becomes necessary.

S

SET

Stay alert and monitor official emergency information. Be prepared to leave immediately.

G

GO

Evacuate when directed. Follow official instructions and use designated evacuation routes whenever possible.

Evacuation Checklist

Keep the following items ready so you can leave quickly if an evacuation becomes necessary.

✓
Emergency water and food supplies
✓
Important identification and documents
✓
Necessary medications and personal items
✓
Flashlights, batteries, chargers, and communication devices
✓
Supplies for pets and service animals
✓
Clothing and essential personal items

Before You Leave

  • Close windows and doors.
  • Move combustible items away from structures when it is safe to do so.
  • Take essential medications and important documents.
  • Bring pets with you.
  • Follow official evacuation instructions.
  • Do not block roadways needed by emergency vehicles.

During an Evacuation

Remain calm and follow directions from firefighters, law enforcement officers, and emergency officials. Traffic may be heavy, and evacuation routes may change as conditions develop.

EVACUATION

Follow Official Orders

If authorities issue an evacuation order for your location, leave immediately.

WARNING

Be Prepared

An evacuation warning means conditions may require evacuation. Prepare to leave without delay.
